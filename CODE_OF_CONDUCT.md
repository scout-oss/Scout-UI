# Code of Conduct

Scout UI is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project
for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementation choices, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Make room for contributors with different communication styles and levels of
  experience.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about safety, scope, and project standards.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through the contact
method listed on that maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue
containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
kept confidential as far as reasonably possible.
