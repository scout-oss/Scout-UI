The official pack is framework-neutral and React-free. Its manifest—not a
manually maintained gallery—is the authority for IDs, tags, dimensions,
transparent bounds, creator, source, license, attribution status, and checksum.

## Use the shipped format honestly

The frozen v0.1 pack ships SVG files. `StickerDefinition` and React rendering
are format-agnostic for SVG, PNG, and WebP, but no raster asset export exists
until a cleared raster asset actually ships.

## Meet the safe SVG contract

Use a stable kebab-case ID, preserve the viewBox, include transparent cut-line
padding, and avoid scripts, event handlers, external resources, `foreignObject`,
unsafe URLs, or embedded private marks. Official artwork already owns its paper
cut line and ink outline; do not add a second wrapper treatment by default.

## Record provenance

Every contribution needs creator/source, license, attribution status, editable
source, transparent bounds, and deterministic checksum. CC0 removes an
attribution requirement; it does not remove the audit trail.

## Know what cannot enter

Do not contribute Scout product logos, screenshots, product claims, third-party
logos, copyrighted characters, or artwork without clear redistribution rights.
Read the repository's asset contribution policy before opening a change.

## Bring your own stickers

Consumers can pass image-like URLs or React content directly to the components.
They do not need to publish a pack or adopt Scout UI artwork to use its bounded
interaction behavior.
