Scout UI ships a recognizable default skin, not a JavaScript theme runtime.
Every component works without Tailwind, CSS-in-JS, or a theme provider.

## Start from semantic foundations

Set variables on `.sui-theme`, `[data-sui-theme]`, or your own ancestor. Keep
paper and night readable, and remap focus independently from decorative accents.

<CodeBlock
  language="css"
  code={`.my-scout-theme {
  --sui-paper: #fffaf0;
  --sui-ink: #17131b;
  --sui-ultraviolet: #6330e8;
  --sui-focus: #005fcc;
  --sui-focus-separation: #fffefa;
}`}
/>

## Keep focus semantic

An accent may become a brand color, but focus, danger, disabled, and
forced-color roles must remain independently legible. Test focus outside hard
shadows on both paper and night surfaces.

## Supply typography locally

Override `--sui-font-display`, `--sui-font-body`, and `--sui-font-mono`. Scout
UI does not download fonts or require Bricolage, Inter, or Geist Mono at
runtime.

## Customize intentionally

Use documented `.sui-*` classes and public `--sui-*` component variables for
meaningful contracts. Do not depend on variables containing `-internal-`; they
belong to imperative engines and may change.
