# Scout UI Official Sticker Art Direction

The first official pack expresses controlled chaos: bold, tactile interruption
held inside a calm and repeatable production system.

## Construction

- Canvas: `160 × 160`, transparent, with at least 8px optical padding.
- Cut line: paper white, approximately 8–10px outside the ink silhouette.
- Inner outline: Scout ink, 4px at source size, with rounded caps and joins.
- Palette: Scout ink and paper plus two to four saturated Scout accents.
- Depth: flat vector artwork; internal offsets may suggest print
  misregistration, but assets contain no baked generic blur shadow.
- Safe size: the primary silhouette remains readable at 32px; internal details
  are no thinner than 3px at source size.
- Complexity: one dominant silhouette, no more than six meaningful internal
  marks, and no decorative detail that disappears before 48px.

## Shape language

Forms are compressed, slightly asymmetric, and deliberately hand-positioned.
Irregularity is bounded: corners wobble and circles are imperfect, but shapes
remain recognizable and never appear broken. Doodle strokes use 3–5px rounded
ink lines.

The v0.1 official pack is vector-only. Photographic/material cutouts are allowed
in later packs only when they share the same paper edge, palette treatment, and
optical weight. Text is prohibited in official v0.1 artwork; labels remain blank
and internationally usable.

Future PNG/WebP exceptions must retain alpha, remain between 32px and 2048px per
axis and below 500KB, and pass the pipeline's fixed lossless Sharp settings.
Raster optimization is deterministic and validated twice in the asset test; v0.1
ships no raster payload.

## Review sizes and surfaces

Every asset is reviewed at 112px, 56px, and 32px on paper, night, and at least
one accent surface. Review checks silhouette, cut line, outline, contrast,
family resemblance, visual weight, transparent bounds, cropping, and dense
collage behavior.
